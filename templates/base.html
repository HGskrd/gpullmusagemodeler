Privacy

This planner stores your current scenario and change history under a random browser identifier so the service can maintain your session and understand how the calculator is used. Scenario snapshots can include hardware, model, cost, and use-case assumptions. With your first visit it also records your approximate country and city (from our CDN), the site that referred you, campaign tags, device type, and browser language; it never stores your IP address. Avoid entering confidential names or data you do not want stored by this service.
