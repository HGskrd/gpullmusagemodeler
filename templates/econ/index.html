How to read these pages

Every variant renders the same underlying projection (your current Config A) — the same views also power the calculator's Demand & economic impact section, grouped into the Flow / Dashboard / Brief / Fleet tabs there. Demand from your use cases is routed to deployed models, and each token lands in one of four fates — served on your GPUs, spilled to cloud for lack of capacity, leaked to cloud for lack of a compatible/affordable model, or destroyed when even cloud exceeds the willingness to pay. Money figures are equivalent value priced at the cloud substitute, not booked revenue. Edit the scenario in the calculator and reload any variant to compare.
